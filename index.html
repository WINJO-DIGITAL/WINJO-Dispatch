How it works

From application to your first booking

  1. You apply

    Fill in the form, read the agreement and tick the two boxes. Sending it creates nothing and charges nothing.

  2. We invoice the set-up fee

    You pay it outside the website. Once it is paid and we have approved you, we create your business and invite your first admin.

  3. You set up your business

    Add your prices, logo, drivers and legal details, write your website, and connect your own Stripe account. Stripe starts in test mode, so you can try a payment before any real money moves.

  4. Customers start booking

    Point your own domain at your website. Requests arrive in the Operator app, you accept or decline them, and accepted customers get a confirmation with a link to pay.

Payments

Your customers pay you, not us

Card payments are taken on your own Stripe account. We never hold the money and we take no percentage of it.

You connect your Stripe account from the Operator app, using Stripe's own sign-up pages. A customer who chooses to pay by card is sent to a Stripe checkout for your business, and the payment is recorded on the booking when Stripe confirms it. Cash bookings are never marked as paid until you do it yourself.

Stripe's fees are charged by Stripe to your account. We do not set them and cannot change them.

We supply software, and nothing else

Winjo Digital is not a private hire operator and is not a party to any of your bookings. Your licences, insurance, vehicles, driver checks and local licensing rules are yours alone, and we do not check any of them. Each operator is responsible for its own compliance. The full wording is in Schedule A.

Questions

Before you apply

Who is this for?

Private hire and transfer businesses that take bookings for airports and other journeys, with an office and drivers of their own. If you run one, it is yours; each business has its own website, prices, team and drivers.

What does it cost?

£999 to set up, paid before we start, then £99 a month from the day your service is live. We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added. You also buy your own domain name from any registrar, in your own name.

Do you take a share of my card payments?

No. Customers pay your own Stripe account directly. Stripe charges its own fees to that account.

Do I need my own Stripe account?

Yes, to take card payments. You create and connect it from the Operator app. Cash bookings work without one.

Whose licences and insurance cover the journeys?

Yours. We supply software only: we are not a private hire operator and do not check any operator's licences, insurance, vehicles or drivers.

Can I use my own web address?

Yes. You buy the domain name and we connect it to your website. Your customers see your name, not ours.

Are customers texted automatically?

No. Texts are prepared for you and sent from your own phone, or a driver's, with one tap. Nothing is sent automatically and we do not charge per text.

What if something goes wrong?

Support is by email at hello@winjodigital.co.uk, 24 hours a day, every day. The agreement sets out what support covers.

What happens when I apply?

We read every application ourselves. If we go ahead, we invoice the set-up fee. Once it is paid and you are approved, we create your business and invite your first admin.
