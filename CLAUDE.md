# Project scope (owner decision, updated 14/11/2026)

This repository is **Winjo Digital's own multi-tenant booking and dispatch platform**: a customer website, an Operator app and a Driver App in one repo (`apps/operator`, `apps/driver`, the website at the root). Other businesses sign up (`/signup`) and each gets its own website, prices, branding, team and drivers.

**Nothing in this project belongs to any earlier product or business.** Only the infrastructure patterns were reused. Never name, default to, build for, or ask about an earlier business. If you find a reference to one (code, tests, docs, comments, defaults, fixtures), remove it or replace it with neutral platform wording: the product name is **Winjo Dispatch**, the company is **Winjo Digital**. Test data uses neutral sample businesses. The earlier standalone repositories are unrelated: never change, deploy or point anything at them. The history of what was removed is in git (commit before `Remove earlier-product references`).

# Working mode

Use the project skills in `.claude/skills/`: `loop-engineering` (work continuously: assess, plan, build, test, fix, review, repeat; keep `docs/TASKS.md` and `docs/HANDOVER.md` current; stop only for a real owner decision) and `anti-ai-design` (any website or visual design work). The rules in this file win over both skills.

# Cost policy (hard constraint, read before doing anything)

**No additional cost of any kind without explicit prior approval from the owner.**

- No new billed resources, ever, without asking first: Supabase branches or projects, plan or add-on upgrades, paid APIs or SaaS, extra compute, paid monitoring, domain purchases, and so on. Even pennies: always ask first.
- Prefer free tiers and already-provisioned infrastructure. Prefer read-only checks plus additive, reversible migrations over a paid test resource.
- **Database backups: never pay for them.** Do not buy, enable or upgrade any paid backup or plan.
- **Twilio SMS is dormant.** All Twilio sending is gated behind `SMS_ENABLED=true`, which is unset. Customer messages go out as two-tap `sms:` texts from a driver's or operator's own phone: the message is stored as a task, the person is push-notified, they open Messages pre-filled and press Send, then tap "Mark as sent". Do not set `SMS_ENABLED`, remove the gate or add Twilio-sending flows without approval.
- Abandon or redesign any plan that turns out to need a new cost.

# Legal wording (hard rule)

**No solicitor or lawyer is used, and none can be afforded.** Terms, privacy policies and any other legal or compliance wording are drafted from, and checked against, official UK sources (legislation.gov.uk, the CMA, the ICO, gov.uk), with the sources, the changes made and the residual risks recorded in `docs/legal/audit-2026-11-12.md`. Never suggest "have a solicitor review this". Say what was checked, what could not be verified and what the owner must do. Not legal advice and not a guarantee.

**The platform is software only.** It is not a private hire operator and not a party to any business's bookings. Licences, insurance, vehicles, driver checks and local licensing law are the operator's alone. Never write a requirement or document that makes the platform responsible for, or implies it checks, any of these. The wording lives in `docs/legal/operator-responsibilities.md` (single source for the operator contract, data terms and the Settings, Legal confirmation `brand.legal.responsibilities_confirmed`). Customer terms name the provider in a final section when `brand.legal.platform_provider` is set (by the platform owner, not the operator).

# Date and time format (hard rule)

**Every date is DD/MM/YYYY. Every time is 24-hour HH:MM, UK time.** Screens, emails, texts (including `sms:` messages), push notifications, PDFs and invoices, subjects, toasts and database templates. Never `YYYY-MM-DD`, US order or a written-out month (one owner-approved exception: the Operator calendar's navigation headings, e.g. "October 2026").
- Plain `YYYY-MM-DD` values are rearranged directly (no `Date` parsing, so no timezone shifts the day). Helpers: `lib/format.js` `fmtDate`, `apps/operator/src/lib/dates.ts`, `apps/driver/lib/format.ts`. In SQL use `to_char(..., 'DD/MM/YYYY')`.
- Timestamps display in `Europe/London`. Native `<input type="date|time">` pickers follow the phone's settings.

# Architecture

- **Multi-tenant database**: `tenants` (slug, name, contact, `brand` jsonb, `pricing` jsonb, Stripe Connect fields, fees), `tenant_users` (roles `super_admin`, `operator_admin`, `dispatcher`, `driver`; `tenant_id` null = platform-wide super admin), `tenant_id` + RLS on every business table, helpers `private.is_super_admin()`, `private.is_member_of_tenant()`, `private.staff_for_tenant()`. A new business is created with `public.provision_tenant()` (SMS, WhatsApp and push start off). A new table needs explicit grants for any role other than `authenticated` and `service_role`.
- **Self-serve sign-up** (`/signup`, `api/signup`, `lib/signup.js`, table `signup_applications`): applicant accepts the Operator Services Agreement and Schedule A (versions in `lib/signup.js`; bump them whenever the documents change), pays the set-up fee outside the app, the platform owner confirms payment and approves in the Operator app (Settings, Platform, Applications), which creates the business. Fees (set-up, monthly) are a record in `platform_fees` / `tenants`; nothing is billed automatically.
- **Website sites per business**: `lib/siteContent.js`, `lib/siteBuilder.js` (design directions classic / editorial / bold), `lib/tenantSite.js`, content in `tenants.brand.site` written by `update_tenant_site()`; photos only from the business's own `brand-assets/<tenant_id>/` folder. Public terms and privacy per business at `/t/<slug>/terms` and `/privacy`, shown only once its legal details are complete and it has confirmed the responsibilities statement.
- **Per-business prices**: `tenants.pricing` `{ airports: [{ name, code?, one_way, return_fare }], extra_stop }`, written only by `update_tenant_pricing()`; the website (`lib/pricing.js`, refreshed at most once a minute) and the Operator app read the same row. A business with no prices gets no built-in list.
- **Per-business branding**: `tenants.brand` `logo_url`, `colors`, `invoice` letterhead; written by `update_tenant_branding()` / `update_tenant_profile()`. Emails use one shared template (`lib/emailTemplate.js`, wording in `lib/emailContent.js`, copy in `apps/operator/src/lib/email/`; keep the copies in step). Queued emails are re-rendered at send time from the booking's current details (`lib/emailQueueRender.js`).
- **Stripe Connect**: each business connects its own Stripe account (Settings, Payments, Stripe-hosted onboarding); card payments are taken directly on that account, the platform takes no percentage and never holds the money. Switched off until `STRIPE_PLATFORM_SECRET_KEY` is set; test mode first, never live without the owner's go-ahead.
- **Payment flow**: a website request waits for Accept/Decline (`operator_response`); an operator-made booking is `accepted` on creation. Accept sends "Booking Confirmed" with a Pay Now link to the booking page, which offers Card (Stripe Checkout, `metadata.bookingId`) or Cash (never marked Paid). `reconcileCheckoutSession()` in `lib/payments.js` is idempotent and shared by the Stripe webhook and `/api/payment/verify`; duplicates and unknown bookings are recorded in `stripe_webhook_events`, bad signatures in `stripe_webhook_rejections`. The webhook function must read the raw request stream (`"config": { "helpers": false }` in `vercel.json`) and the signing secret must be a `whsec_` value. A return trip is one booking with the whole fare plus a £0 return leg; accepting or declining applies to both with one email; a return leg is never payable on its own.
- **Cancellation policy**: version in `lib/cancellation.js` (`TERMS_VERSION`, `BANDS`, `cancellationQuote`); the booking form requires an unticked-by-default acceptance; the Booking Confirmed email states the booking's own deadlines. Keep `lib/cancellation.js`, the terms page, the prices page table, the FAQ and the booking-form box in step.
- **Reminders**: a daily cron picks bookings by UK calendar date; week-ahead (5 to 7 days) and day-before reminders go by email and as two-tap texts handed to the assigned driver. The driver sees the text generated at the moment of tapping, so it is never stale.
- **Partners**: `drivers.kind` is `driver` (uses the Driver App) or `partner` (trusted third party, no login). Partner jobs' two-tap texts go to the office. The Operator app's Today screen lists "Texts to send".
- **Security hardening**: app roles hold no TRUNCATE/REFERENCES/TRIGGER; anonymous visitors only INSERT on `contact_messages` and `quote_requests` and SELECT on `reviews`; drivers see only their own assigned jobs for their own business and can set `No Show` only from En Route or Arrived, never `Cancelled`; a driver's login id must equal their `drivers.id` (the invite flow does this). `anon` EXECUTE on `get_push_dispatch_bundle` and `authenticated` USAGE on `invoice_seq` must stay. Customers can read but not write their own profile row. Database tests: `bash scripts/db-test.sh` (throwaway Postgres 17 built from the baseline; pgTAP) and in CI (`.github/workflows/db-tests.yml`).
- **Operator push alerts**: new-booking and no-show database triggers post to `https://winjo-dispatch.vercel.app/api/push/dispatch` with the shared secret from `push_config`; nothing is sent while `push_config` has no row.
- **Tests**: website `npm run test:unit`; Operator app `npm test`; Driver App `npm test` (and `npm run test:functions`).
