## Three deliberate differences from `schema.sql`
1. `notify_driver_job_update()` and `notify_driver_new_booking()` called the **old project's** edge functions (`https://yoltkmhtxwluqxxpewbl.supabase.co/functions/v1/...`) with booking details. They now point at this project's own address. Those edge functions do not exist here yet, so the calls return 404 (harmless, asynchronous).
2. `private.run_evexec_backup()` and its `revoke` were **not installed**. It read every table and the auth users and posted them to a different project (`xrcrwcejxkcowhrfbmgq`) with a hardcoded key. It could not have run (its vault secret is missing and no schedule is created), and it does not belong on this platform.
3. Supabase's own `public.rls_auto_enable()` is also present (installed on every new project, not part of the baseline).

## Still pointing at the retired business (leftovers, listed in docs/TASKS.md)
- The first-business row `ev-exec` (id `00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000001`) exists because column defaults and the sign-up trigger use it. The owner chose to keep it for now.
- `notify_operator_new_booking()` and `notify_operator_no_show()` post to `https://evexecoperator.vercel.app/api/push/dispatch`. They only fire when `push_config` holds a webhook secret, and it is empty, so nothing is sent until the platform's real address is set.

- Function names `evexec_esc` and `evexec_notification_email`.
- `pg_net` was enabled without naming a schema, so the advisor shows it in `public`. It is not relocatable without dropping it.
